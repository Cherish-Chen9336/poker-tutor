    How a hand works

    Every hand follows the same steps. There are up to four betting rounds, one after each new set of cards.

      A hand can end at any moment: if everyone else folds, the last player left wins the pot without showing any cards.

      Hand rankings and odds

      Your hand is the best five cards out of seven: your two hole cards plus the five shared cards. Each hand beats every hand below it. The odds show how often seven cards make that hand if you stay to the river.

      Chance with 7 cards (Hold'em)

        Surprised? With seven cards, you make one pair (43.8%) and two pair (23.5%) more often than nothing at all (17.4%). That is why a single pair is often not enough to win.

        These odds are for Texas Hold'em, where you pick your best five from seven cards. Tables for five-card poker look very different (for example, high card is 50.1% there) because fewer cards give fewer chances to make a pair or better.
